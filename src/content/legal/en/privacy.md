## 4. The OfflineHandwerk app {#app}

### Local storage

Everything you record in the app, for example calculations, customer, employee and business data and your company logo, is stored only locally on your device. We have no access to it and do not transfer it to our own servers. The app does not create an automatic cloud backup of these contents. On Android it does not take part in system backup (Auto Backup). You yourself are responsible for the lawfulness of processing this data.

The app contains no crash-reporting service and no analytics SDK of its own. It creates no usage profiles of your work. Text you enter, form contents, calculations and screen contents are not collected.

### Permissions

The app accesses your photo library only when you select a company logo. The app does not access the camera or the microphone.

### In-app purchases

Purchases are handled through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google are independently responsible for processing the payment data.

To manage and unlock purchased features we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). For that purpose RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous ID together with purchase and transaction data. Content from the app, such as calculations or customer data, is not transmitted. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because the processing is necessary in order to provide you with the purchased features. For transfers to the United States, RevenueCat's contractual terms (Data Processing Addendum) provide for EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). How long this data is kept is set out under [Retention](#retention).

### Evaluation of purchase data {#analytics}

The controller for this processing is Jan Siems, Hintergasse 28, 67308 Bubenheim, Germany, reachable as stated in the [legal notice](/en/imprint/).

In addition to unlocking purchases, we evaluate data at RevenueCat only to this extent: the aggregated number of paying customers via the status of the entitlement offlinehandwerk_premium, and the duration of app use. Text you enter, form contents, calculations and screen contents are not collected. There is no profiling and no advertising. We do not use a further SDK for this.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is knowing the number of paying customers and the duration of app use. The recipient and processor is RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). Google Play is a recipient of the purchase data from payment processing. Transfers to the United States use the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in RevenueCat's Data Processing Addendum named above (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). The retention period is set out under [Retention](#retention). Your rights are in section 5. You request deletion at RevenueCat as described under [Deleting your data](#deletion).

### Sharing and export

If you share, print or email PDFs or backups, the app hands the file to the app or service you select. We do not receive this data.

With Premium you can back up and restore the local data in the app. The export creates an unencrypted JSON file containing customer, calculation and business data. Purchase receipts are not included. You choose where to keep the file. We do not upload it.

### Retention {#retention}

Local data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, delete the app data, reset the app or uninstall it.

Data at RevenueCat, that is the pseudonymous ID, the purchase history (including purchase token, product and dates) and the subscription status, is kept for the term of the contract with RevenueCat. On request we delete it immediately via Delete Customer, in the dashboard or with DELETE /subscribers/{app_user_id}. At the end of the contract it is returned or deleted under Section 7 of the Data Processing Addendum, subject to RevenueCat's backup practice. You request deletion as described under [Deleting your data](#deletion).

We keep Google payout and earnings reports (tax records) for 8 or 10 years. Eight years apply to accounting vouchers (Buchungsbelege) and ten years to books and annual financial statements (§ 147 AO, § 257 HGB). The period starts at the end of the calendar year.

Purchase and transaction data that Google or Apple store themselves follow their own rules: [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Deleting your data {#deletion}

There is no user account. You delete app contents on the device: in the app, by deleting the app data or resetting the app, or by uninstalling the app. We do not hold these contents and cannot delete them remotely. Files you have already exported or shared, such as PDFs and backups, stay with the recipient or at the location you chose.

RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) processes purchase data for the Premium subscription. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous ID and purchase and transaction data, not the contents of the app. Google Play Billing or Apple handles the payment itself. To ask for deletion of the purchase data stored at RevenueCat, email [info@offlinehandwerk.de](mailto:info@offlinehandwerk.de). If the app shows an identifier in subscription management, include it.

We then delete the related customer record immediately via Delete Customer in the RevenueCat dashboard or with DELETE /subscribers/{app_user_id}. RevenueCat's public documentation says that this removes the purchase history RevenueCat stores for that customer. RevenueCat describes this as sufficient for a GDPR erasure request. At the end of the contract the data is returned or deleted under Section 7 of the Data Processing Addendum, subject to RevenueCat's backup practice. The periods are set out under [Retention](#retention). Details of deletion are in [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy-policy).

Deleting the app or the record at RevenueCat does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play or the Apple App Store. Purchase records that Google or Apple keep themselves follow their own rules; see [Retention](#retention).

### Provision of personal data

Providing personal data is neither a statutory nor a contractual requirement. Without connection data, the website cannot be delivered; without purchase data, purchased features cannot be unlocked. No automated decision-making, including profiling, takes place.

## 5. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR) and data portability (Art. 20 GDPR).

### Right to object

You have the right, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to object at any time to the processing of your personal data based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. If you object, we will no longer process the data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (Art. 21(1) and (4) GDPR).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz, Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz.
